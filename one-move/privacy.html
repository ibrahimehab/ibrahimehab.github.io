Privacy Policy for One Move

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game One Move (the "app") uses and how. The app is published by Ibrahim Ehab ("I", "me"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no online profile.
  • Your game progress stays on your phone.
  • The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and Google collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information I collect

I do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or precise location, and I do not run my own servers that receive data from the app.

Your levels completed, best scores and settings (sound, vibration, motion) are saved only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to me. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or using "Reset all progress" in Settings erases them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show advertisements: full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in return for a hint or extra moves.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process information from your device, which can include:

DataWhy
Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)Show ads, limit how often you see an ad, and (where you allow it) personalise ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itServe ads for your region, detect fraud and measure ads
Device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version)Make ads display correctly, diagnostics and analytics
Ad interactions (ads viewed, clicked or completed)Measure ad performance and pay for rewarded ads

This information is collected by Google and its advertising partners under their own privacy policies. I do not receive your advertising ID or the other data above. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice any time in the app under Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • Android settings: You can opt out of ads personalisation, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in Settings > Google > Ads on your device.
  • Non-personalised ads: If you decline personalised ads you may still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

Children

One Move is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact me and I will help resolve it.

Sharing and selling data

I do not sell your personal information. The only data sharing is the advertising data exchange with Google AdMob described above.

Data retention and deletion

I hold no personal data about you. Progress saved on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. To ask Google to delete or export data it holds about you, use your Google Account data and privacy controls.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because I do not hold personal information about you, most requests need to go to Google through the links above. You can also contact me with any request.

Security

The app does not transmit your progress anywhere, and ads are loaded over secure (HTTPS) connections.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy, for example if the app adds features. The effective date above will change, and for significant changes I will also note it in the app update description.
